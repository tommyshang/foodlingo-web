FoodLingo

Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

FoodLingo ("the app") is made by NSISUS ("we"). FoodLingo reads restaurant menus from photos and explains each dish in your language. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it.

What we collect

  • Account details. If you sign in, we store your email address and a user ID. With Google sign-in we also receive your name and email from Google. If you continue as a guest, we create an anonymous user ID with no personal details.
  • Menu photos. Photos you take or choose are uploaded to private storage tied to your user ID so they can be translated. For signed-in users we keep them so they appear in your scan history on all your devices. If you use the app as a guest, we delete your photos from our storage within 7 days. The app keeps its own copy on your phone and uploads it again only if it is needed.
  • Scan history. For signed-in users, the translated menus you scan are saved to your account so they appear on all your devices. Guest history stays on your phone.
  • Preferences. Your app language, translation language, and any allergies and foods to avoid you enter. For signed-in users these are saved to your account. Allergy information is used only to flag dishes for you.
  • Purchases. If you subscribe to FoodLingo Plus, Google Play handles payment. We receive your subscription status and purchase history, not your card details.

The app does not use advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services, and it does not track you across other apps or websites. Text-to-speech runs on your device.

How we use it

  • To translate and explain the menus you scan.
  • To keep your history and preferences in sync when you sign in.
  • To give subscribers unlimited scans.
  • To answer you if you contact support.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Who processes your data

We use these service providers only to run the app:

  • Supabase: sign-in, database, and photo storage (servers in the United States).
  • Google Cloud: hosts our server that handles menu scans.
  • Google Gemini API: reads the menu photo and writes the translation. Photos are sent only to process your scan.
  • Google Play and RevenueCat: process and keep track of subscriptions.
  • Google Sign-In: if you choose to sign in with Google.

How long we keep it

For signed-in users, we keep your account data, including menu photos, until you delete your account. Deleting your account removes your account details, menu photos, scan history, and preferences from our systems. If you use the app as a guest, your menu photos are deleted from our storage within 7 days of upload. Google Play and RevenueCat keep purchase records as required for billing, tax, and fraud prevention.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account in the app under Profile → your account card → Delete Account, or ask us to do it without the app. See Delete your FoodLingo account.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Photos are kept in private storage that only your account can access, and database rows are restricted to their owner.

Children

FoodLingo is meant for adults (18+) and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
