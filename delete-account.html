FoodLingo

Delete your FoodLingo account

FoodLingo is made by NSISUS. You can delete your account and its data in the app, or by email if you no longer have the app.

In the app

  1. Open FoodLingo and tap Profile.
  2. Tap your account card at the top.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account and data are deleted right away.

By email

Email foodlingo.support@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my FoodLingo account", from the email address you signed in with. We will confirm by email and delete your account within 30 days.

Guest use

If you used FoodLingo as a guest and never signed up, there is no account to delete. Menu photos from guest use are deleted from our storage automatically within 7 days of upload. Your guest history stays on your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and user ID)
  • Your menu photos
  • Your scan history
  • Your preferences, including allergies and foods to avoid

What is kept

Google Play and RevenueCat keep subscription purchase records as required for billing, tax, and fraud prevention. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Data stored only on your phone, such as guest history, is removed when you uninstall the app.
